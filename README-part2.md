## Where My Coworkers Can Get Better

### HD Phone Communication

* **Garden** and sometimes **Lot** has disappeared from HD group.
* A quick **“On my way”** confirms that a request has been heard.
* Good phone communication helps the store work as one team.

### Customer Calls — Need a Service-Ticket System

* Many customers depend on phone calls to reach an aisle associate or manager.
* Most at Poway are responsive, but follow-through is not always consistent.
* A simple **service-ticket system** could record the request, assign ownership, and track whether the customer received a response.
* This would improve accountability and customer service without depending on repeated calls or verbal messages.
